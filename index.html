STEP 1 — MAP YOUR INTAKE

List every channel: phone, web form, email, walk-in, referral. For each, record: timestamp received, timestamp first contacted, who responded, outcome. Most shops discover 30-50% of leads never get a first touch.

STEP 2 — TIME THE GAP

The killer metric is median time-to-first-contact. Under 5 minutes = you win. 5-30 minutes = coin flip. Over 30 minutes = you lost. Pull 30 days of data and compute it.

STEP 3 — FIND THE BOTTLENECK

Is it after-hours? Is it one person drowning? Is it no system at all? The audit forces the answer into the open instead of letting it hide in "we're busy."

STEP 4 — PLUG THE HOLE

Auto-respond within 60 seconds on every channel. Route by zip code and trade type. Log every attempt. Re-contact at 24h and 72h. This is not software you buy — it's a loop you run.
